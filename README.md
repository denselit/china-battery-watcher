# China Battery Technology Watcher

## Overview

China Battery Technology Watcher is a personal research assistant system designed to monitor technological developments in the Chinese battery industry.

The system focuses on:

- CATL
- BYD
- LFP battery technology
- Lithium-rich manganese (LMR)
- Sodium-ion batteries

The goal is not to replace human technical judgment.

Instead, it helps researchers:

- collect important information
- reduce information overload
- avoid missing key technology developments


---

## Project Philosophy

AI should augment human researchers, not replace them.

This system does not attempt to predict:

- which company will win
- which technology will dominate
- investment decisions

It focuses on:

"Finding the right information at the right time."
